# Kopi 25 Ribu Sehari — treatment (channel "Uang & Kerja", episode 1)

Style: Spreadsheet Film. Vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, ~40 s. Indonesian voice (Gemini TTS, id-id-podcaster-1), burned-in subtitles as a cell comment.
Unsur: hitung-hitungan + andaikan + solusi (pain: "uang habis entah ke mana"). Pure arithmetic, no external facts.

## Three structures
1. **Receipt that grows**: one row per day fills down the sheet for a year. Honest, but slow and repetitive for a short.
2. **The `####` gag ladder (chosen)**: each step multiplies the cell; the yearly total is too wide for its column and shows `####`; the column opens on the voice. Then a five-year count is drawn as cells (one cell = one cup), and the turn subtracts cups instead of quitting coffee.
3. **Two columns duel**: "ngopi tiap hari" vs "2× seminggu" side by side from the start. Clear, but no surprise: the answer is visible before the question.
Chosen: 2, because the `####` gag is the native turn of the medium and the surprise lands on a number.

## Logline and arc
A Rp25.000 coffee typed into one cell grows, step by step, into Rp45 juta in five years — and the fix is not to quit, but to delete five cups a week.
Setup: one small cell. Turn: `####` → 6,6 juta, then the five-year wall of cups. Ending: cups greyed out one by one, saving Rp6,5 juta a year; the question to the viewer.

## Benchmark
Office-software "music videos built in a spreadsheet" (grammar: cells as pixels, formula bar as dialogue). Learn: one formula per beat, colour = rule. Don't take: any product UI, logo, ribbon, font or colour scheme.

## Cue map
112 BPM office-pop: kit, electric bass, marimba, glockenspiel. Intro sparse (marimba + hats), groove from the `####`, cut to silence 0.6 s before the 5-year reveal, full groove on the paint sweep, half-time under the fix, final stab on the end card.

## Sound design
Key clicks per typed character, Enter clack + thump, tick per selection hop, ratchet on column widen, rising sine blips per painted cell (grouped), whoosh on the pull-out, ding on the saving result.

## Subtitles and type
Inter. Subtitles = pale yellow cell comment at the lower left, one Indonesian line, never wrapped (long lines split into several cues). Numbers on screen ≥ 26 px at the zoom they are read.
